Getting Started

Everything you need to know to track your grades like a pro.

Sync with Canvas
Install GradeCalc Sync Pro from the Chrome Web Store. Pin it, open Canvas, and click Update Synced Grades.
Build Your Transcript
Click Add Old Subjects in the sidebar to manually enter past classes not synced from Canvas.
Grade Simulator
Select an active class and enter hypothetical scores to see exactly how your final grade changes.
Target Calculator
Find out exactly what you need on your next exam to hit your goal grade.
Custom Grading Scales
Click the gear icon next to any subject to set a custom scale (e.g. A = 94%, AB = 89%).
Curve Adjustments
Click the trending-up arrow on completed classes to apply a professor curve to your final grade.
