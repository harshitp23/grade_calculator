Getting Started

×

1. Syncing with Canvas

Install GradeCalc Sync Pro from the Chrome Web Store. Pin it, open Canvas, and click "Update Synced Grades" to import your classes.

2. Building Your Transcript

Click Add Old Subjects in the sidebar to manually enter classes from previous semesters not in Canvas. Your GPA trend graph will automatically update!

3. Grade Simulator

Select an active class, and type in hypothetical scores to see exactly how your final percentage changes.

4. Target Calculator

The Target Calculator tells you exactly what score you need on your next exam/assignment to hit your goal.

5. Custom Grading Scales

Click the gear icon next to any subject on your dashboard to set a custom grading scale so your GPA is calculated perfectly.

6. Curve Adjustments

For completed classes, click the trending up arrow icon to apply a manual curve adjustment.
