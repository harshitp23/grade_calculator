Getting Started 🚀

×

1. Syncing with Canvas

The easiest way to get your grades into GradeCalc is using our Chrome Extension.

• Install GradeCalc Sync Pro from the Chrome Web Store.
• Pin the extension to your browser toolbar.
• Log into your university's Canvas page.
• Click the extension icon and hit Update Synced Grades to instantly import your active classes and assignments!

2. Building Your Transcript

Your dashboard tracks your Cumulative GPA. To make it accurate, click Add Old Subjects in the sidebar to manually enter classes you completed in previous semesters. Your GPA trend graph will automatically update!

3. Grade Simulator

Wondering how a bad test score will affect your final grade? Go to the Grade Simulator, select an active class, and type in hypothetical scores to see exactly how your final percentage changes.

4. Target Calculator

If you want to keep an 'A', the Target Calculator tells you exactly what score you need on your next exam to hit your goal. It handles all the complex weighted math for you.
