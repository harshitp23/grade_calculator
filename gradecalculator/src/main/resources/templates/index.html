Getting Started 🚀

×

1. Syncing with Canvas

Install GradeCalc Sync Pro from the Chrome Web Store, pin it, log into Canvas, and click Update Synced Grades.

2. Building Your Transcript

Click Add Old Subjects to manually enter past classes and keep your GPA accurate.

3. Grade Simulator

Enter hypothetical scores to see how your final grade will change.

4. Target Calculator

Find out exactly what you need on your next exam to hit your goal grade.

5. Custom Grading Scales

Click the gear icon to set a custom scale for any class.

6. Curve Adjustments

Click the trending-up arrow on completed classes to apply a professor curve.
