Getting Started

Everything you need to know to track your grades like a pro.

Sync with Canvas
Install GradeCalc Sync Pro from the Chrome Web Store. Pin it, open Canvas, and click Update Synced Grades.
Auto-Sync
Toggle Auto-Sync in the extension popup and your grades update automatically every time you visit Canvas — no clicking needed.
Build Your Transcript
Click Add Old Subjects in the sidebar to manually enter past classes not synced from Canvas.
Grade Simulator
Select an active class and enter hypothetical scores to see exactly how your final grade changes.
Target Calculator
Find out exactly what you need on your next exam to hit your goal grade. Available on the dashboard and as a floating widget inside Canvas — click the 🎯 button on any Canvas page.
In-Canvas Calculator
A draggable 🎯 button appears on every Canvas page. Click it to open a mini Target Calculator — it auto-detects your course so you can calculate scores without leaving Canvas.
Custom Grading Scales
Click the gear icon next to any subject to set a custom scale (e.g. A = 94%, AB = 89%).
Curve Adjustments
Click the trending-up arrow on completed classes to apply a professor curve to your final grade.
